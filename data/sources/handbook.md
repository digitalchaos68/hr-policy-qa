# Acme Pte Ltd — Employee Handbook
*This is a fictional handbook created for demo purposes only. It does not represent a real company.*

## Annual Leave
Confirmed employees are entitled to 16 days of annual leave per calendar year, prorated for employees who join or leave mid-year. This exceeds the statutory minimum under the Employment Act. Leave must be applied for at least 3 working days in advance via the HR portal and is subject to manager approval. Unused leave of up to 7 days may be carried forward to the following year and must be used by 31 March, after which it lapses.

## Sick Leave
Employees who have completed at least 3 months of service are entitled to 14 days of paid outpatient sick leave and 60 days of paid hospitalisation leave per year (inclusive of outpatient sick leave), in line with the Employment Act. A medical certificate (MC) from a company-approved clinic or a registered medical practitioner must be submitted within 24 hours of the first day of absence. Employees with less than 3 months of service are entitled to sick leave on a prorated basis per the statutory schedule.

## Parental and Childcare Leave
Eligible employees (Singapore Citizen child, employee has served at least 3 months) may apply for Government-Paid Childcare Leave of up to 6 days per year for children under 7 years old. Female employees are additionally entitled to Government-Paid Maternity Leave of up to 16 weeks. Male employees may apply for up to 4 weeks of Government-Paid Paternity Leave. Acme tops up unpaid infant care leave with 2 additional company-paid days per year as a discretionary benefit beyond the statutory entitlement.

## Working Hours and Overtime
Standard working hours are 9:00am to 6:00pm, Monday to Friday, with a 1-hour lunch break, totalling 44 hours per week. Employees covered under Part IV of the Employment Act (generally those earning up to the prevailing salary cap) are entitled to overtime pay at 1.5 times the hourly basic rate for work beyond 44 hours per week, capped at 72 overtime hours per month. Non-Part IV employees are not entitled to statutory overtime pay but may be granted time-off-in-lieu at management's discretion.

## Work-From-Home (WFH) Policy
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to team and manager approval. WFH days must be logged in the HR portal by 9:00am on the day itself. Employees are expected to remain contactable during core hours (10:00am–4:00pm) and attend all scheduled meetings via video call. This is a company policy with no equivalent statutory requirement.

## Public Holidays
Acme observes all 11 gazetted public holidays in Singapore. Employees required to work on a public holiday will receive an extra day's salary at the basic rate or a day off in lieu, in accordance with the Employment Act.

## Expense Claims
Business-related expenses (transport, client meals, approved training) may be claimed via the Finance portal within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims must be supported by original receipts. Claims exceeding SGD 200 require prior manager approval before the expense is incurred. Acme does not reimburse alcohol, entertainment beyond client-facing meals, or personal expenses.

## Notice Period
The notice period during probation is 1 week. Upon confirmation, the notice period is 1 month for employees below manager grade, and 2 months for manager grade and above, unless otherwise stated in the employment contract. Notice may be served in writing and either party may offer to pay salary in lieu of notice, subject to mutual agreement.

## Probation
New employees serve a probation period of 3 months from their date of joining, which may be extended by up to 3 additional months at management's discretion if performance requires further evaluation. Confirmation is communicated in writing by HR upon successful completion.

## Flexible Benefits
Confirmed employees receive an annual flexible benefits allowance of SGD 800, which may be used for wellness (gym membership, fitness classes), approved health screenings, or professional certification exam fees. Unused allowance does not carry forward and is not convertible to cash.

## Grievance Handling
Employees with a workplace grievance should first raise the matter informally with their direct manager. If unresolved within 2 weeks, the matter may be escalated in writing to HR, who will investigate and respond within 10 working days. Employees may request confidentiality during the process, subject to the needs of a fair investigation.

## Retrenchment Benefits
In the event of retrenchment, employees who have served at least 2 years will receive retrenchment benefits of 2 weeks' salary per year of service, in line with prevailing Tripartite Advisory guidelines. This is a company practice rather than a statutory entitlement under the Employment Act, as Singapore law does not mandate a fixed retrenchment benefit formula for all employees.
